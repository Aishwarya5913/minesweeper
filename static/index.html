📖 How to Play Minesweeper

🎯 The Objective

The goal of Minesweeper is to uncover all the safe squares on the board without detonating any hidden mines!

🎮 How the Controls Work

  • Left Click / Tap: Reveals a covered square.
  • Right Click / Long Press: Places or removes a red/gold flag 🚩 on suspected mines.
  • Flag Mode Button: If on mobile or trackpad, click Flag Mode to switch left clicks between uncovering and flagging!
  • Double Click / Chord: Clicking an already revealed number with all its neighboring flags placed will instantly clear remaining adjacent safe squares!

🔢 Reading the Numbers

When you uncover a safe square, it reveals a number from 1 to 8. This number indicates exactly how many of the 8 directly touching squares (horizontal, vertical, diagonal) contain a mine.

1 Touching 1 Mine
2 Touching 2 Mines
3 Touching 3 Mines

💡 Winning Strategy & Tips

  • Guaranteed Safe Start: Your very first click is guaranteed to be 100% safe and will open a generous cleared area.
  • Look for Corners: A '1' located in an isolated corner only has one available neighbor, meaning that neighbor is definitely a mine!
  • Satisfied Numbers: If a '1' already has one flagged mine touching it, all other neighbors around that '1' are completely safe to uncover.
  • Use the Hint Button: Stuck on a difficult board? Click the 💡 Hint button to analyze the board with logical deduction!
